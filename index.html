About Kirchhoff Lab

Kirchhoff Lab is a free place to practise Kirchhoff's circuit rules. Solve randomly generated circuits, check your answers, and follow a step-by-step worked solution for every circuit.

The two rules

Junction rule (Kirchhoff's current law, KCL): the currents flowing into a junction add up to the currents flowing out. It comes from conservation of charge.

Loop rule (Kirchhoff's voltage law, KVL): going once around any closed loop, the voltage changes add up to zero. It comes from conservation of energy.

How to practise

Pick one of four levels, from a single loop up to three loops and five currents. Every circuit has whole-number answers. Find each current and check your answers, or peek at one when you're stuck.

The worked solution goes one step at a time: choosing current directions and loop directions, writing the junction and loop equations, solving the system of equations, and checking the result with a voltage map and an energy check.

For teachers

Every circuit has a code, such as L2-42. Share its link and the whole class gets the same circuit. It works on a projector, in light or dark mode, with IEC or US resistor symbols.
